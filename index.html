Install on your phone

Turn QuestForge into a home-screen app.

  1. Publish the app folder to a secure HTTPS address.
  2. Open that address in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  3. Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu.
  4. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  5. Open QuestForge from the new home-screen icon.
This starter app works offline after it has been hosted securely and loaded once, thanks to its service worker. Progress is saved on the device where you use it.
